# CS 255 DriverPass Project

## Project Overview

The DriverPass project involved designing a system for a client named DriverPass that wanted to improve driver training by offering students online practice exams and behind-the-wheel driving lessons. The client needed a system that would allow customers to create accounts, purchase training packages, schedule driving appointments, and track their progress. The system also needed administrative features for managing instructors, appointments, and customer information.

## Reflection

**What did you do particularly well?**

I believe I did particularly well identifying and organizing the client's business requirements and translating them into a system design. I worked to ensure that the system's functionality aligned with the client's expectations, particularly regarding user account management, appointment scheduling, and the different responsibilities of customers, instructors, and administrators.

**If you could choose one part of your work to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?**

If I could revise one part of my work, I would focus on improving the system design diagrams. Although the diagrams communicate the overall structure and functionality of the system, I would add more detail to demonstrate how individual components interact with one another. I would also refine the diagrams to make them easier for developers and stakeholders to understand.

**How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it important to consider the user's needs when designing?**

I interpreted the user's needs by reviewing the DriverPass interview and identifying the specific functions the client expected from the system. I used those requirements to guide my system design, ensuring that customers could access training materials and schedule appointments while administrators and instructors could manage their respective responsibilities. Considering user needs is important because software should solve real problems and provide a positive experience for the people using it.

**How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?**

My approach to software design begins with understanding the problem and identifying the requirements before making decisions about implementation. I believe it is important to break larger systems into smaller components and use diagrams to visualize how those components interact. In the future, I would continue using UML diagrams, requirement analysis, and feedback from stakeholders to develop systems that are functional, maintainable, and scalable.
